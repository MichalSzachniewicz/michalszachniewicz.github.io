I am a Benjamin Peirce Fellow at Harvard University mentored by [Laura DeMarco](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~demarco/). During the academic year 2025–2026, I was staying at the Institute for Advanced Study. I received my PhD from the University of Oxford, where I was supervised by [Ehud Hrushovski](https://weizmann.elsevierpure.com/en/persons/ehud-hrushovski/). Previously, I was a Master's student at the University of Warsaw, supervised by [Joachim Jelisiejew](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/~jjelisiejew/), and a Bachelor's student at the University of Wrocław, under the supervision of [Krzysztof Krupiński](https://www.math.uni.wroc.pl/~kkrup/).

I spend a lot of time thinking about globally valued fields. These are fields equipped with an extra data called height functions. Such arise from Arakelov intersection thoery, or an adelic curve structure on a field. In 2026 I was awarded the Zoé Chatzidakis [award](https://sciencesmaths-paris.fr/en/our-programs/programmes-hommages/phd-thesis-award-zoe-chatzidakis) for my PhD thesis which was centered around fields with heights.

For a nice overview of the theory of globally valued fields, see the Bourbaki Seminar [talk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20120) given by [Antoine Chambert-Loir](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~antoine.chambert-loir/index.xhtml).
